# User Discovery Interview 2

> **Privacy Notice**: Real names, emails, and student IDs omitted per course guidelines.

- **Interviewee Role**: Junior Biology / Pre-Health Student, Gonzaga University
- **Interviewer Role**: Student Systems Analyst
- **Date**: Fall 2026
- **Context**: Hemmingson Center study lounge

---

## Background & Current Habits
- Heavy memorization and lab report preparation (Organic Chemistry, Physiology).
- Studies 4–6 hours daily, typically split between silent floor library sessions and late-night dorm sessions.
- Struggles with phone distractions (TikTok, group chats) breaking focus cycles.

---

## Interview Notes & Observations

- **Previous attempts**: Tried Notion templates downloaded from Reddit. Spent an entire Sunday afternoon customizing relational databases, color-coding tags, and creating study progress bars.
- **Pain points**:
  - "I spent 4 hours building the Notion template, used it for two days, and then completely stopped because updating it between classes was too slow on mobile."
  - "If an app requires me to sign in with an email and password every time I open my laptop, I will close the tab and never come back."
- **Willingness to commit**: Paid $3.99 for the Forest mobile app a year ago. Liked that the phone was locked down, but noticed it didn't track total weekly subject hours or link to actual study sessions on her laptop.
- **Workarounds**: Uses simple sticky notes on the corner of the laptop screen with tally marks for completed 25-minute study intervals.
- **Key takeaway**: Any system must require zero login friction, offer quick 25-minute Pomodoro-style interval support, and provide an immediate visual summary of daily hours per course without manual spreadsheet data entry.
